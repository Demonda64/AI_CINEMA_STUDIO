# Prompts Grok - Itération et analyse

Prompts pour utiliser Grok comme outil d'analyse, de reformulation et de contrôle qualité.

## Analyse de cohérence visuelle

**Prompt** :
`Analyze these generated frames for a minimalist psychological horror short film. Check whether the visual continuity is coherent: same bedroom, same blue-gray nocturnal palette, same tired male protagonist, mirror anomaly subtle and human. Identify any continuity breaks, over-explicit horror elements, or shots that feel too stylized. Return concise notes and suggested fixes.`

## Amélioration de prompt HeyGen

**Prompt** :
`Improve this HeyGen video prompt while preserving the creative constraints: realistic minimalist psychological horror, blue-gray desaturated bedroom, subtle mirror anomaly, no monster, no gore, no jump scare, slow controlled movement. Make the prompt clearer and more likely to produce restrained cinematic motion.`

## Variantes du reflet

**Prompt** :
`Generate three restrained variations for a mirror anomaly in a psychological horror short. The reflection must remain human and almost normal. The anomaly should come from gaze, posture, timing, or a faint smile. Avoid monsters, deformation, gore, jump scares, and supernatural effects.`

## Analyse audio

**Prompt** :
`Analyze this sound design plan for a minimalist psychological horror short. Check whether the sound remains subtle, domestic, and non-musical. Identify anything that feels too trailer-like, too supernatural, or too explanatory. Suggest restrained improvements.`

## Décision de sélection

**Prompt** :
`Compare these generated video versions for the same shot. Rank them for narrative clarity, visual continuity, psychological subtlety, and suitability for a 4-5 minute minimalist horror short. Recommend one selected version and explain why in concise production notes.`
