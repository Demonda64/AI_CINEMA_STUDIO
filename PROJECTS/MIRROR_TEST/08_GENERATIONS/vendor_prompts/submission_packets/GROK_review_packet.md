# Packet Grok - Revue prompts et cohérence

## Objectif

Utiliser Grok pour challenger les prompts HeyGen et vérifier que les sorties restent cohérentes avec le projet.

## Prompt 1 - Revue des prompts HeyGen

`Review these two HeyGen prompts for a minimalist psychological horror short. The visual rules are: realistic nocturnal bedroom, blue-gray desaturated palette, subtle mirror anomaly, human reflection, no monster, no gore, no jump scare, slow controlled movement. Improve the prompts only if needed, and keep the horror restrained. Return concise revised prompts and a list of risks.`

## Prompt 2 - Analyse de sortie vidéo

`Analyze this generated video for continuity and tone. Check: blue-gray palette, minimalist bedroom, human reflection, subtle anomaly, slow movement, no explicit monster, no gore, no jump scare. Identify any issue that would break the film's psychological horror tone. Recommend: retain, retouch, or reject.`

## Prompt 3 - Variantes du reflet

`Generate three restrained alternatives for the mirror anomaly. The reflection must stay human and almost normal. The difference can come from gaze, posture, timing, or a faint smile. Avoid deformation, monsters, gore, jump scares, supernatural effects, or theatrical horror.`
