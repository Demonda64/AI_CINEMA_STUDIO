# Prompts HeyGen - Vidéo

Prompts pour générer ou animer les plans vidéo clés avec HeyGen.

## Direction globale

Créer des vidéos courtes, réalistes, lentes et retenues. Le film doit rester psychologique : pas de monstre, pas de gore, pas de jump scare, pas de caméra spectaculaire.

## Prompts par plan

### S01-P02 - Réveil 3:17

**Prompt** :
`Animate this shot subtly: a digital alarm clock showing 3:17 in a dark blue-gray bedroom. Keep the camera almost still, add only a very slight push-in and tiny red LED flicker. No hands, no person, no supernatural effect.`

**Durée cible** : 5 secondes.

### S02-P03 - Message

**Prompt** :
`Animate this smartphone message shot subtly. The phone is held in a dark bedroom, cold screen light, message readable: "Tu aurais dû le laisser branché." Add a very slight hand tremor and minimal screen glow fluctuation. No extra text, no new notification, no face reveal.`

**Durée cible** : 7 secondes.

### S04-P01 - Première anomalie du reflet

**Prompt** :
`Animate the man facing the mirror in a dark minimalist bedroom. The real man remains tense and almost still. The reflection is human and nearly identical, but its gaze slowly becomes too direct, as if it notices the viewer before the man does. Keep the movement subtle and realistic. No monster, no deformation, no jump scare.`

**Durée cible** : 10 secondes.

### S04-P04 - Révélation

**Prompt** :
`Animate the close mirror reflection. The reflected face remains human, tired and pale, with a direct stare and a faint impossible smile that slowly becomes clearer. The movement must be extremely subtle, almost still. No scream, no mouth opening, no demonic face, no gore.`

**Durée cible** : 12 secondes.

### S05-P02 - Fin ouverte

**Prompt** :
`Animate the final mirror shot in a dark bedroom. A faint human presence remains in the reflection, almost motionless. Add a very slow fade into darkness and minimal room stillness. No explanation, no monster, no sudden movement.`

**Durée cible** : 8 secondes.

## Contraintes globales

- realistic psychological horror
- blue-gray desaturated palette
- dim practical lighting
- slow controlled movement
- no gore
- no explicit monster
- no jump scare
- no saturated colors
- no dramatic camera move
- no text changes unless requested
